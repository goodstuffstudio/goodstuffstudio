Lilt Privacy Policy

Lilt is {{LILT_ONE_LINER}}, made by Goodstuff Studio. It is built to work without knowing who you are.

Effective August 15, 2026 Last updated August 15, 2026

The short version. Lilt has no accounts and no sign-in. Your content stays on your device (and in your own iCloud, if you turn on sync). We have no server that receives it, and no way to read it. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data Lilt collects

This table matches the privacy label shown on Lilt's App Store listing.

Category Collected? Notes
Contact info No No account, no email required to use the app.
Your content No Stored on your device, and in your private iCloud if sync is on. Never sent to us.
Identifiers No No advertising ID, no device fingerprinting.
Usage & analytics No No analytics SDK is linked into the app.
Diagnostics Only if you opt in Apple's crash and performance reports, shared with us in aggregate only if you enabled "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.
Location No Lilt does not request location access.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in Lilt is written to storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes that data. We recommend keeping a device backup — because we hold no copy, we cannot restore anything for you.

iCloud sync

If you turn on sync, Lilt stores your data in your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. That data belongs to your Apple Account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Goodstuff Studio cannot see or access it. Turning sync off stops new data from being written; to remove what is already there, delete Lilt's data from Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Purchases

Any purchase or subscription in Lilt is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple tells the app whether a purchase is active; it does not give us your name, payment method, or card details, and we never see them. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by its own privacy policy.

Third parties

Lilt links no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only outside parties involved are Apple — for the App Store, iCloud, and optional system crash reports — and, if you email us, our email provider.

Children

Lilt is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Since the app collects nothing and requires no account, there is nothing for us to hold.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete personal information a company holds about you. We hold none from the app itself, so there is nothing for us to look up, export, or erase — your copy is on your device and under your control. If you have emailed support, that thread is the one record that exists, and we will delete it on request. Write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If Lilt starts doing something new with data, this policy will say so before that version ships, and the date at the top will change. Every revision is public in the site repository.
