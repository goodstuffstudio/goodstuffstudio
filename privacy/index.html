Privacy

Goodstuff Studio builds software that collects as little about you as possible. This page covers the studio and this website. Each app has its own policy.

Who we are

Goodstuff Studio ("Goodstuff Studio", "we", "us") is the developer of the apps listed above. For anything in this policy, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party trackers, and has no login or forms.

Like any web host, GitHub receives standard request data when you load a page — your IP address, browser user agent, and the page requested — and uses it to serve the site and protect against abuse. We do not receive, store, or have access to those logs. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Email you send us

If you email support, we receive your address, your message, and anything you choose to attach (such as a screenshot or a diagnostic log). We use it only to answer you and to fix the problem you reported. We keep support email while it is useful for that, and delete it on request.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your personal information for advertising.
  • We do not build advertising or cross-app profiles about you, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example under the GDPR in the UK and EEA, or the CCPA/CPRA in California — you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because we hold so little, most requests amount to deleting a support email thread. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will respond within 30 days. Exercising these rights costs you nothing and we will not treat you differently for it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of the page. Material changes will also be noted in the app or on this site before they take effect. The full history of every change is public in the repository for this site.
