Reporting a bug

The more of this you can include, the faster we can fix it — but send what you have, even if it is just "this looked wrong":

  • Which app, and the version number.
  • Your device and OS version.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if the problem is visual.

Requesting a feature

Tell us the problem you are trying to solve rather than the solution you have in mind — it usually leads somewhere better. We read every request, and we keep a list, though we cannot promise a timeline.

Refunds and billing

Purchases and subscriptions made through the App Store are handled by Apple, not by us — we cannot see your payment details or issue a refund directly. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple declines and you think that is wrong, email us and we will see what we can do.
